# Retail Sales & Profitability Analysis | Excel Dashboard

### Turning retail sales data into actionable business insights.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project explores the sales and profitability performance of a simulated multinational retail business using Microsoft Excel.

The analysis focuses on understanding the factors affecting profitability, identifying business performance trends, and exploring opportunities for improvement.

An interactive dashboard was developed to present key findings and allow users to explore performance across different years and regions.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The analysis aims to answer key business questions:

- Is revenue growth translating into increased profitability?
- Which products and regions contribute most to business performance?
- How are discounts affecting profit margins?
- What impact do supplier costs, operating expenses, and product returns have on profitability?
- How can historical sales data help forecast future revenue?
- What business decisions could improve profitability?

## 📈 Interactive Dashboard

The dashboard brings the major findings together in one place.

It includes key performance indicators, charts, and visualisations covering sales, profitability, discounts, costs, returns, and forecasts.

Users can interact with the dashboard using **Year and Region filters** to explore different aspects of business performance.
